# Returns and refunds policy — draft for owner review

This is a draft policy for the Al Rafy Jewels demo website and is not a final business commitment.

Return eligibility, time limits, refund timelines, exchange rules and conditions for damaged items must be confirmed by the business owner before launch.

The AI assistant must not promise or approve a refund. Customers should submit a complaint with their order reference and the store team will review the request.
