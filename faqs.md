# Frequently asked questions

Al Rafy Jewels sells jewellery items listed in the current product catalogue. Product prices are shown in Pakistani rupees (PKR).

For product availability, check the current catalogue or contact customer care. Sample catalogue stock is for development and must be verified before accepting real orders.

For questions about a specific item, include the product name in your message. Do not assume that a product is solid gold or a genuine gemstone unless its listing explicitly says so.
